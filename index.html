I foster joy for humanity.

The dance of tech, art & people make my heart sing.

I was born in Taipei. I studied CS at Berkeley. I live in San Francisco now, in the first Associate Product Builder cohort at LinkedIn.

Night markets taught me warmth. Living rooms taught me how to host. I keep five things close: a strong body, a quiet mind, the people I love, a reason to get up, and making someone else’s day lighter.
